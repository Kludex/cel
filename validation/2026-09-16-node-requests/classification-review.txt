**Recommendation:** Reorder `src/node.zig:589-609` so `snapshotMap()` classifies proxies, branded Maps, and plain objects before `OptionalValue` `instanceof`.

- This is observable hardening, not a transparent optimization. `napi_instanceof` executes mutable `OptionalValue[Symbol.hasInstance]`; plain objects and Maps will no longer trigger its exceptions, side effects, or reentrant evaluations.
- Current order can accept `Proxy<OptionalValue>` before `snapshotMap()` rejects proxies. It can also misclassify a real Map when `Symbol.hasInstance` returns true or its prototype is changed to `OptionalValue.prototype`. Intrinsic Map brand should win.
- Normal `OptionalValue` subclasses remain `.other` and reach `instanceof`. Wrappers changed to `Object.prototype` or `null` are already treated as records today.
